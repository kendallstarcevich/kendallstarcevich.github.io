> My project and final presentation for my 2025 Data Analyst Internship with Corteva Agriscience.

# Project Overview
During my internship at **Corteva Agriscience**, I designed and deployed three Power BI dashboards for different projects. I worked with the **Monday.com API** and **Databricks** (Spark, Parquet files, and Jobs) to build ETL pipelines, transform raw data, and model relationships within **Power BI**. I implemented DAX measures to calculate KPIs, documented methodologies, and automated data refreshes using **Power Automate** and **DevOps** workflows. The dashboards were published to SharePoint, providing **real-time, accessible project tracking and governance insights.**
