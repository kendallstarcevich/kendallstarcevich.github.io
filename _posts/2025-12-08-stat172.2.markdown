> models for a 'client' who is interested in both understanding the relationships in the data and being able to predict future observations

**This is the final project for my Data Mining and General Linear Models Statistics Course.** We were tasked to use **R** to make

1. A prediction tool that can be used to predict a 2-level (equivalently, binary) categorical outcome on
new data
2. A parsimonious GLM that can be used to help understand important relationships between the
explanatory variables and your Y variable.
3. Stupendous, meaningful data visualizations that strengthen findings of predictive and descriptive models.

with the final models to be put into **automated production to aid in both forecasting and understanding.**
